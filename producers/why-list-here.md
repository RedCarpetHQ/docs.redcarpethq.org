# Why List Your IP on RedCarpetHQ

## Beyond Crowdfunding: Finance Your Film Like a Financial Asset

Traditional crowdfunding treats your film like a charity case. Investors get perks, not positions. RedCarpetHQ treats your IP as a financial asset — because that is what it is.

---

## The Producer Value Proposition

### 1. Zero Platform Fees on Your Raise

We do not take a percentage of your funding. You receive **100% of every USDC raised**.

| Platform | Campaign Fee | You Keep |
|---|---|---|
| Kickstarter | 5% + 3–5% processing | ~90% |
| Indiegogo | 5% + 3% processing | ~92% |
| Seed&Spark | 5% | 95% |
| **RedCarpetHQ** | **0%** | **100%** |

We earn from secondary market trading, not from your budget.

### 2. A Global Capital Pool, Not Just Your Network

Traditional crowdfunding relies on your personal network. RedCarpetHQ opens your project to:
- DeFi yield seekers looking for non-correlated assets
- Entertainment investors who want liquidity
- Global retail participants with no minimum investment
- Crypto-native communities who understand token economics

### 3. Passive Income from Secondary Markets

Every time your token trades, you earn **10% of the trading fee** — forever.

```
Example:
- Your film token trades $2,000,000 in volume
- Trading fees collected: $50,000 (2.5%)
- Your producer share: $5,000 (10%)
- This repeats every month, quarter, year — automatically
```

No invoicing. No accounting. Smart contract distribution.

### 4. Liquidity for Your Supporters = More Demand for Your Token

Investors are reluctant to lock capital for years. On RedCarpetHQ:
- They can trade tokens anytime on the marketplace
- They can lend USDC against tokens for yield
- They can use tokens as collateral to borrow

This liquidity premium means investors are willing to commit more capital, knowing they are not trapped.

### 5. Transparent Profit Distribution

When your film generates revenue, you distribute dividends via on-chain snapshots:

1. Deposit USDC into DividendDistributor
2. Snapshot captures all token holders at that block
3. Holders claim their pro-rata share
4. No lawyers, no waterfall spreadsheets, no delays

Your investors see exactly where the money goes. Trust is automated.

### 6. All-or-Nothing Protection for Both Sides

Your campaign only succeeds if it hits its **Floor** (minimum goal). If not, everyone is refunded automatically. This protects:
- **You**: No underfunded productions. You either have enough budget or you try again.
- **Investors**: Capital is only committed to viable projects.

### 7. No Creative Control Surrendered

Your token represents economic participation, not creative control. You retain:
- Final cut authority
- Casting and hiring decisions
- Distribution strategy
- Artistic direction

Investors receive financial upside, not a producer credit (unless you choose to offer one).

## Who Should List

### Ideal Projects
- Feature films with budgets of $100K–$5M
- TV series seeking season financing
- Documentaries with distribution partnerships
- Animation and genre projects with strong fanbases
- International projects seeking cross-border capital

### Requirements
- Original entertainment content (film, TV, documentary, animation)
- Clear budget breakdown
- Realistic Floor (minimum viable budget)
- Transparent revenue plan (streaming, theatrical, licensing)
- Pass screening process (if screening enabled)
