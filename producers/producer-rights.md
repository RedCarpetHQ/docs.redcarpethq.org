# Producer Rights & Protection

## What You Control, What Protects You

Listing your IP on RedCarpetHQ does not mean surrendering control. This document clarifies your rights, protections, and obligations as a producer.

---

## What You Control

### Creative Control

Your token holders are economic participants, not creative partners. You retain full authority over:

- **Final cut** and editorial decisions
- **Casting, crew, and hiring**
- **Distribution strategy** (theatrical, streaming, festival circuit)
- **Marketing and publicity**
- **Sequels, spin-offs, and derivative rights**

Token holders receive dividends if you choose to distribute. They do not receive veto power over creative decisions.

### Financial Control

- You set the **Floor** (minimum budget) and **Ceiling** (maximum raise)
- You choose the **overage type** (fixed, unlimited, or capped)
- You set the **token price**
- You decide **when and how much** to distribute in dividends
- You receive **100% of funds raised** (no platform fee)

### Campaign Control

Before your campaign starts, you can edit:
- Start time, end time
- Floor and ceiling amounts
- Funds recipient address

After launch, you can:
- **Cancel** the campaign anytime (full refunds to all investors)
- **Extend** once (if within 24-hour grace period)
- **Claim funds** after successful funding (within 2-week finalize window)

---

## What Protects You

### All-or-Nothing Funding

If your campaign does not hit its Floor by the deadline, it fails. All USDC is automatically refunded. This protects your reputation:

- No underfunded productions that cannot deliver
- No angry investors who backed a project that limped forward
- Clean separation: success or reset

### 2-Week Finalize Grace Period

After hitting your Floor, you have **2 weeks** to decide whether to claim funds or cancel. This gives you time to:
- Review the final investor list
- Confirm legal compliance
- Consult with your team and financiers
- Decide if proceeding is the right move

After 2 weeks, anyone can finalize the campaign — preventing stalled projects.

### No Platform Fee

Unlike every other platform, we do not take a cut of your raise. This means:
- Your Floor can be lower (you need less to hit your true minimum)
- Your Ceiling can be higher (more capital reaches your production)
- You keep every dollar for your project

### Producer Fee Income

You earn **10% of all trading fees** on your token — forever. This creates:
- Passive income from secondary market activity
- Alignment with long-term token health
- Incentive to maintain investor relationships post-release

### Burn-for-Vault Exit

If you hold tokens and want to exit without crashing the market:

- Burn your tokens
- Receive protocol vault shares (no market sell pressure)
- Minimum threshold: $10 USDC equivalent
- 25% exit fee applies (protects the ecosystem)

## Your Obligations

### Honest Representation

- Your budget breakdown should be realistic
- Your timeline should be achievable
- Your revenue projections should be grounded
- You must communicate delays or changes via Project Updates

### Dividend Transparency

If you promise revenue sharing in your campaign materials, you are expected to follow through. The blockchain does not forget:
- Clearly state your intended revenue-sharing formula
- Communicate when revenue is expected
- Distribute when revenue materializes
- If no revenue occurs, communicate why

### No Market Manipulation

- Do not wash-trade your own token
- Do not make false claims to pump price
- Do not exploit the RiskOracle with fake volume

Violations can result in:
- Risk tier downgrade (higher costs, lower limits)
- Contest disqualification
- Platform delisting in extreme cases

## The Screening Process

If screening is enabled, you must be whitelisted by a screener before creating campaigns. This is not censorship — it is quality control:

- Protects investors from scams
- Maintains platform reputation
- Preserves decentralization (multiple screeners, not one gatekeeper)
- Can be toggled off if the community prefers open access

---

## Legal Considerations

### Intellectual Property

- You must own or control the IP you are financing
- Clearly state rights ownership in campaign materials
- Be transparent about co-production partners or licensors

### Securities Compliance

RedCarpetHQ tokens represent **project participation and profit-sharing**, not equity or securities. However:
- Consult legal counsel in your jurisdiction
- Do not promise guaranteed returns
- Do not market to accredited-only audiences (we are permissionless)
- Be clear that tokens are speculative and carry risk

### Tax

- Funds raised are typically treated as income or project financing
- Trading fees you receive are income
- Consult a tax professional
