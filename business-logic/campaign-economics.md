## Key Economic Principles

### Funding Mechanics

**All-or-Nothing Model:**
- Campaign must reach goal to succeed
- Funds held in escrow until graduation
- Automatic refunds if goal not met
- Grace period for final push

### Token Economics

**1:1 Token Ratio:**
- 1 USDC contribution = 1 token
- Simple and transparent
- Fair distribution
- No pre-mine for producers

### Fee Structure

**5% Platform Fee:**
- Deducted when producer withdraws
- Distributed to ecosystem
- Funds platform development
- Rewards community participants
