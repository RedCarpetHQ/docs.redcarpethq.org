# Screening Process

RedCarpetHQ uses a screening system to ensure quality campaigns and protect the community. This guide explains how to get whitelisted as a producer.

## Why Screening?

### Quality Control
- Prevents spam and low-quality campaigns
- Ensures producers are serious and committed
- Protects supporter investments
- Maintains platform reputation

### Fraud Prevention
- Verifies producer identity
- Reviews project legitimacy
- Checks for red flags
- Reduces scam risk

### Community Trust
- Builds confidence in campaigns
- Encourages supporter participation
- Creates sustainable ecosystem
- Attracts serious filmmakers

## Screening Status

The screening requirement can be toggled by platform administrators:

- **Enabled** (Default): Only whitelisted producers can create campaigns
- **Disabled**: Open access for all producers

**Current Status:** Check platform homepage or contact support

## Who Are Screeners?

Screeners are trusted addresses authorized to:
- Review producer applications
- Whitelist approved producers
- Remove whitelist status if needed
- Cancel problematic campaigns

**Screener Responsibilities:**
- Verify producer credentials
- Assess project viability
- Check legal compliance
- Monitor campaign quality

## Application Process

### 1. Prepare Your Application

**Required Information:**
- **Producer Details**
  - Full name
  - Production company (if applicable)
  - Contact information
  - Wallet address (Ethereum)

- **Project Information**
  - Film title and genre
  - Synopsis (500 words max)
  - Budget breakdown
  - Timeline and milestones

- **Credentials**
  - Previous film work (IMDb links)
  - Industry experience
  - Awards/recognition
  - Team credentials

- **Legal Documents**
  - Business registration (if applicable)
  - Identity verification
  - Terms acceptance

### 2. Submit Application

**Email Submission:**
```
To: screening@redcarpethq.org
Subject: Producer Screening Application - [Your Name]

Body:
- Producer information
- Project details
- Credentials
- Wallet address: 0x...
- Attachments: Supporting documents
```

**Platform Submission** (if available):
1. Visit RedCarpetHQ
2. Click "Apply as Producer"
3. Fill out application form
4. Upload documents
5. Submit for review

### 3. Review Period

**Timeline:** 3-7 business days

**What Happens:**
1. Screener receives application
2. Reviews credentials and project
3. May request additional information
4. Makes whitelist decision
5. Notifies applicant

**Possible Outcomes:**
- ✅ **Approved**: Wallet whitelisted, can create campaigns
- ⏳ **Pending**: Need more information
- ❌ **Rejected**: Does not meet criteria (can reapply)

### 4. Whitelist Confirmation

**If Approved:**
1. Receive confirmation email
2. Wallet address whitelisted on-chain
3. Can immediately create campaigns
4. Access producer dashboard

**Verification:**
Check whitelist status:
```
Visit: RedCarpetHQ → Connect Wallet → Check Status
Or: Contact support with wallet address
```

## Screening Criteria

### Project Viability

**Assessed Factors:**
- Clear project vision
- Realistic budget
- Achievable timeline
- Market potential
- Team capability

**Red Flags:**
- Vague project description
- Unrealistic goals
- No production experience
- Incomplete information

### Producer Credibility

**Positive Indicators:**
- Verified identity
- Industry track record
- Professional presentation
- Transparent communication
- Legal compliance

**Concerns:**
- Anonymous identity
- No verifiable experience
- Poor communication
- Legal issues
- Previous fraud

### Legal Compliance

**Requirements:**
- Comply with local regulations
- Proper business structure (if required)
- Intellectual property rights
- No legal disputes
- Terms of service acceptance

### Financial Responsibility

**Considerations:**
- Realistic funding goals
- Clear fund usage plan
- Budget transparency
- Financial accountability
- Revenue sharing commitment

## Whitelist Removal

### Voluntary Removal
Producers can request whitelist removal:
1. Contact screener or support
2. Provide reason (optional)
3. Confirm removal
4. Lose campaign creation access

### Involuntary Removal

**Reasons:**
- Campaign fraud
- Terms violations
- Supporter complaints
- Legal issues
- Inactivity (case-by-case)

**Process:**
1. Screener identifies issue
2. Investigates situation
3. Contacts producer
4. Makes removal decision
5. Executes on-chain removal

**Appeal:**
- Email: appeals@redcarpethq.org
- Provide explanation
- Submit evidence
- Wait for review decision

## Screener Actions

### Campaign Cancellation
Screeners can cancel campaigns if:
- Fraud detected
- Terms violated
- Producer unresponsive
- Legal issues arise
- Community safety threatened

**Effect:**
- Campaign immediately cancelled
- Supporters can claim refunds
- Producer loses access to funds
- Whitelist may be removed

### Emergency Powers
In extreme cases, screeners can:
- Pause campaigns
- Freeze operations
- Coordinate with authorities
- Protect supporter funds

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How long does screening take?**  
A: Typically 3-7 business days, depending on application completeness.

**Q: Can I apply without film experience?**  
A: Yes, but you must demonstrate project viability and commitment. First-time filmmakers are considered.

**Q: What if my application is rejected?**  
A: You can reapply after addressing the concerns. Contact screeners for feedback.

**Q: Is there an application fee?**  
A: No, screening is free.

**Q: Can I create campaigns while pending?**  
A: No, you must be whitelisted first.

**Q: How many campaigns can I create after approval?**  
A: Unlimited, as long as you maintain good standing.

**Q: Can my whitelist be revoked?**  
A: Yes, for violations of terms or fraudulent activity.

**Q: Do I need a production company?**  
A: No, individual filmmakers can apply.

**Q: What if screening is disabled?**  
A: You can create campaigns immediately without approval.

**Q: Can I have multiple wallet addresses whitelisted?**  
A: Contact screeners to discuss your specific needs.
