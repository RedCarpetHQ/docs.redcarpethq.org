# Creating Trading Offers

For detailed information about creating and managing trading offers, please see:

- [Marketplace Overview](trading/marketplace.md) - Complete trading guide
- [Token Trading](tokenomics/trading.md) - Trading mechanics and strategies

## Quick Guide

### Creating a Sell Offer

1. **Navigate to marketplace**
2. **Select your token**
3. **Click "Create Offer"**
4. **Enter details:**
   - Token amount
   - Price per token (USDC)
   - Expiration (optional)
5. **Approve token spending** (first time)
6. **Confirm transaction**

### Offer Management

**View Your Offers:**
- Active offers
- Fill status
- Total value locked

**Cancel Offers:**
- Return tokens to wallet
- Pay gas fees
- Instant cancellation

**Partial Fills:**
- Buyers can purchase portions
- Remaining tokens stay in escrow
- Offer stays active

For complete details, visit the [Marketplace Overview](trading/marketplace.md) and [Token Trading](tokenomics/trading.md) pages.
