## 🌐 What is RedCarpetHQ?

RedCarpetHQ is a **decentralized exchange and DeFi protocol purpose-built for entertainment IP**. Every successful campaign becomes a tradable ERC-20 token with its own lending vault, marketplace, and automated risk engine.

### The Big Idea

Traditional entertainment financing is opaque, illiquid, and gatekept. We replace it with:

- **Tokenized IP Rights** — Each project gets a liquid ERC-20 token representing economic participation
- **Native Secondary Markets** — No waiting for distribution. Trade positions anytime on-chain
- **Embedded DeFi** — Every token carries a UnifiedVault (ERC-4626) for USDC lending/borrowing, automatically risk-priced by our AI oracle
- **Algorithmic Trust** — RiskOracleV3 monitors price integrity, wash trading, and vault health in real time
- **Automated Profit Distribution** — Revenue from streaming, box office, and licensing flows directly to token holders via snapshot-based dividends

### Key Differentiators

| Legacy Crowdfunding | RedCarpetHQ |
|---|---|
| Backers get perks or gratitude | Investors get tradable, yield-bearing tokens |
| No secondary market | Escrow marketplace with VWAP price discovery |
| No financial upside for backers | Dividends, lending APY, and trading profits |
| Manual trust in creator | On-chain risk scoring and circuit breakers |
| Platform takes 5-12% of raise | Zero campaign fees; revenue from trading & interest |

## 🔐 Security & Trust Architecture

- **Screener Whitelist**: Human-in-the-loop quality control without centralization
- **RiskOracleV3**: Real-time AI-driven risk assessment (GREEN/YELLOW/RED tiers)
- **Circuit Breakers**: Automatic protocol pause on bad debt >$10k, utilization >99%, or stale pricing >12h
- **ReentrancyGuard & Pausable Contracts**: Industry-standard smart contract protections
- **4-Layer Protection**: Collateral → Stability Pool → Insurance Fund → Protocol Treasury
