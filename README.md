# RedCarpetHQ Producer Documentation

Welcome to **RedCarpetHQ** - the decentralized platform for movie producers to raise funds, engage with supporters, and build sustainable film financing through blockchain technology.

## 🎬 What is RedCarpetHQ?

RedCarpetHQ is a Web3-powered movie crowdfunding platform that enables producers to:

- **Launch tokenized fundraising campaigns** for film projects
- **Engage with a global community** of film enthusiasts and investors
- **Access DeFi lending** using campaign tokens as collateral
- **Trade tokens** on an integrated marketplace
- **Distribute dividends** to token holders from film revenues
- **Participate in trading competitions** to earn rewards

## 🎯 Platform Highlights

### Flexible Fundraising Models
- **Floor & Ceiling Targets**: Set minimum and maximum funding goals
- **Overage Options**: Choose how to handle excess funding
- **Time Extensions**: Extend campaigns if needed (with limits)

### DeFi Integration
- **ERC4626 Vaults**: Industry-standard lending pools for each token
- **Risk-Tiered Interest**: Dynamic rates based on market health (GREEN/YELLOW/RED)
- **Auto-Liquidation**: Stability pools protect lenders automatically

### Trading Ecosystem
- **Escrow-Based Marketplace**: Safe peer-to-peer token trading
- **Trading Contests**: Compete for fee rewards every 6 hours
- **VWAP Price Oracle**: Fair market pricing with manipulation resistance

### Revenue Sharing
- **Dividend System**: Distribute film revenues to token holders
- **Snapshot-Based**: Fair distribution using blockchain snapshots
- **Multi-Round Support**: Multiple dividend distributions over time

## 🔐 Security & Trust

- **Screener Whitelist**: Quality control through producer screening
- **Risk Oracle**: Real-time risk assessment for all tokens
- **Circuit Breakers**: Automatic safety mechanisms for extreme conditions
