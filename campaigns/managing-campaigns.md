## Campaign Modifications

### Updatable Parameters

**Before Campaign Starts:**
- ✅ Start time
- ✅ End time
- ✅ Floor amount
- ✅ Ceiling amount
- ✅ Funds recipient
- ✅ Campaign description
- ✅ Images and media

**During Active Campaign:**
- ✅ End time (extend)
- ✅ Funds recipient
- ✅ Campaign description
- ✅ Updates and media
- ❌ Token name/symbol
- ❌ Token price
- ❌ Floor/ceiling
- ❌ Overage type

### Extending Deadline

**When to Extend:**
- Close to floor but need more time
- Strong momentum continuing
- External factors delayed promotion
- Community requests extension

**Extension Process:**
1. Campaign must be in grace period
2. Navigate to campaign management
3. Click "Extend Campaign"
4. Set new end date
5. Confirm transaction
6. Announce to supporters

**Limitations:**
- Maximum 1 extension (default)
- Cannot exceed 270 days total
- Must be within grace period
- New date must be future

**Example:**
```
Original End: Feb 28, 2025
Raised: $45,000 / $50,000 (90%)
Grace Period: Mar 1, 2025

Extension:
New End: Mar 31, 2025 (30 days)
Reason: "Just $5k away! Let's finish strong!"
```

### Changing Funds Recipient

**Why Change:**
- Switch to multi-sig wallet
- Update company wallet
- Correct address error
- Security reasons

**Process:**
1. Campaign management dashboard
2. Click "Update Recipient"
3. Enter new address
4. Verify carefully
5. Confirm transaction

**Important:**
- ⚠️ Verify address multiple times
- ⚠️ Test with small amount first
- ⚠️ Cannot recover if wrong
- ⚠️ Update before finalization

## Team Management

### Adding Collaborators

**Roles:**
- **Creator**: Full control
- **Collaborator**: Can post updates, respond
- **Viewer**: Read-only access

**Adding Process:**
1. Campaign management
2. Click "People" tab
3. Click "Add Collaborator"
4. Enter wallet address
5. Select role
6. Confirm transaction

**Use Cases:**
- Co-producers
- Marketing team
- Community managers
- Production assistants

### Removing Collaborators

**Process:**
1. Campaign management
2. "People" tab
3. Find collaborator
4. Click "Remove"
5. Confirm transaction

**Effect:**
- Immediate access revocation
- Cannot post updates
- Cannot manage campaign
- Creator always retains control

### Claiming Funds

**After Success:**
1. Campaign reaches floor
2. Deadline passes or ceiling reached
3. Wait for finalization
4. Click "Finalize Campaign"
5. Funds sent to recipient address

**Timing:**
- Immediate after finalization
- No waiting period
- No manual approval needed
- Automatic USDC transfer

**Verification:**
```

From: Campaign Contract
To: Funds Recipient
Amount: $50,000 USDC
Status: Confirmed
Block: 12345678
```

## Crisis Management

### Handling Issues

**Common Problems:**

**1. Slow Funding**
- Increase marketing
- Post more updates
- Engage community
- Consider extension
- Analyze feedback

**2. Negative Comments**
- Respond professionally
- Address concerns directly
- Provide evidence
- Stay calm
- Learn from feedback

**3. Technical Issues**
- Document problem
- Contact support
- Communicate to backers
- Provide updates
- Follow up when resolved

**4. Team Problems**
- Handle privately first
- Update if affects project
- Be transparent
- Maintain professionalism
- Focus on solutions

### Cancellation Decision

**When to Cancel:**
- Project no longer viable
- Cannot deliver promises
- Legal/regulatory issues
- Force majeure
- Ethical concerns

**Cancellation Process:**
1. Assess situation thoroughly
2. Consult team and advisors
3. Prepare announcement
4. Navigate to campaign management
5. Click "Cancel Campaign"
6. Confirm transaction
7. Post explanation
8. Supporters get refunds

**Communication:**
```
Subject: Important Update - Campaign Cancellation

Dear Supporters,

After careful consideration, we've made the difficult 
decision to cancel this campaign.

Reason: [Honest explanation]

What happens next:
- Campaign cancelled immediately
- Full refunds available
- No fees charged
- Claim anytime

We sincerely apologize and thank you for your support.

[Detailed explanation and future plans]
```
