# Campaign Extensions

> **Note:** Campaign extensions are a planned feature for future platform updates.

## Overview

Campaign extensions allow producers to extend their campaign duration under specific circumstances. This feature is designed to give campaigns additional time to reach their funding goals while maintaining fairness and transparency.

## Coming Soon

This feature is currently under development. Check back for updates on:

- Extension eligibility criteria
- Extension duration limits
- Community voting mechanisms
- Fee structures for extensions

## Current Workaround

If your campaign is approaching its deadline and you haven't reached your goal:

1. **Communicate with supporters** - Keep them informed about your progress
2. **Increase marketing efforts** - Use the remaining time effectively
3. **Consider the grace period** - Campaigns have a grace period after the deadline
4. **Plan for next steps** - Prepare for either success or refund scenarios
