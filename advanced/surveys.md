# Surveys & Governance

## Overview

Surveys and governance mechanisms will allow producers to gather feedback from token holders and enable community participation in campaign decisions.

## Planned Features

### Token Holder Surveys

**Gather community input:**
- Poll token holders on decisions
- Weighted by token ownership
- Transparent results
- Binding or advisory votes

**Use cases:**
- Creative decisions (casting, locations, etc.)
- Budget allocation
- Timeline adjustments
- Reward modifications

### Governance Proposals

**Community-driven decisions:**
- Token holders propose changes
- Voting period
- Quorum requirements
- Execution if passed

**Potential governance areas:**
- Campaign modifications
- Revenue distribution
- Producer accountability
- Platform improvements

### Voting Mechanisms

**Planned voting systems:**
- One token = one vote
- Quadratic voting (future)
- Delegation (future)
- Time-weighted voting (future)

## Current Workarounds

Until surveys and governance are implemented:

**For Producers:**
- Use Twitter polls
- Email surveys to supporters
- Community forums
- Direct communication

**For Token Holders:**
- Engage in community channels
- Provide feedback directly
- Participate in discussions
- Vote with your wallet (buy/sell)
