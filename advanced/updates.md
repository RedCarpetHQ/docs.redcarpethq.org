# Updates & Communication

## Overview

Keeping supporters informed is crucial for campaign success. This guide covers best practices for communicating updates, managing expectations, and maintaining engagement throughout your campaign and beyond.

## Types of Updates

### Campaign Progress Updates

**During funding phase:**
- Funding milestones reached
- New supporters welcomed
- Days remaining reminders
- Stretch goals announced
- Media coverage shared

**Frequency:** 2-3 times per week during active funding

### Production Updates

**After graduation:**
- Pre-production progress
- Filming updates
- Post-production milestones
- Distribution news
- Festival submissions/results

**Frequency:** Weekly to bi-weekly

### Financial Updates

**Revenue and dividends:**
- Revenue reports
- Dividend announcements
- Financial transparency
- Budget updates
- Expense breakdowns

**Frequency:** Quarterly or as needed

### Community Engagement

**Building relationships:**
- Behind-the-scenes content
- Team introductions
- Q&A sessions
- Polls and surveys
- Thank you messages

**Frequency:** As appropriate

## Update Best Practices

### Content Guidelines

✅ **Good updates include:**
- Clear subject/title
- Specific information
- Visuals when possible
- Next steps/timeline
- Call to action (if any)

✅ **Effective structure:**
1. Hook/headline
2. Key information
3. Details/context
4. Visuals/media
5. Next steps
6. Thank you/sign-off

❌ **Avoid:**
- Vague statements
- Overpromising
- Ignoring problems
- Inconsistent communication
- Jargon without explanation

### Tone and Style

**Professional yet personal:**
- Be authentic
- Show personality
- Maintain professionalism
- Admit mistakes
- Celebrate successes

**Transparency:**
- Share good and bad news
- Explain delays honestly
- Provide context
- Don't hide problems
- Build trust

### Timing

**When to update:**
- Major milestones
- Significant changes
- Regular intervals
- Before deadlines
- After important events

**When NOT to update:**
- Nothing new to share
- Just to meet schedule
- During crises (wait for clarity)
- Late at night (unless urgent)

## Handling Different Situations

### Good News

**Celebrating success:**
- Share enthusiastically
- Thank supporters
- Provide details
- Include visuals
- Look ahead

**Example:**
> "🎉 We've reached 75% of our funding goal! Thanks to 150 amazing supporters, we're well on our way to making this film a reality. Only 5 days left - let's finish strong!"

### Challenges and Delays

**Addressing problems:**
- Be honest and direct
- Explain what happened
- Outline solutions
- Provide new timeline
- Apologize if appropriate

**Example:**
> "Update on Production Timeline: We've encountered unexpected delays in securing our primary filming location. We're actively working with alternative venues and expect to finalize by next week. This may push our production start by 2 weeks, but we're committed to delivering the quality you deserve."

### Asking for Help

**Community support:**
- Be specific about needs
- Explain why it matters
- Make it easy to help
- Show appreciation
- Follow up on results

**Example:**
> "We need your help! We're looking for filming locations in the Seattle area. If you know of any interesting spaces or have connections, please reach out. Your local knowledge could be invaluable!"

### Dividend Announcements

**Financial distributions:**
- Announce in advance
- Explain calculation
- Provide timeline
- Include claim instructions
- Answer common questions

**Example:**
> "Dividend Announcement: We're excited to distribute $25,000 in profits from our theatrical run! Dividends will be available to claim starting Monday. Token holders will receive proportional distributions based on their holdings. Full details and claim instructions in the link below."

## Crisis Communication

### If Things Go Wrong

**Immediate response:**
1. Acknowledge the issue
2. Gather facts
3. Communicate quickly
4. Be transparent
5. Outline next steps
6. Follow up regularly

**Don't:**
- Go silent
- Make excuses
- Blame others
- Overpromise fixes
- Delete criticism

### Managing Criticism

**Responding to negative feedback:**
- Stay professional
- Listen to concerns
- Address valid points
- Correct misinformation
- Take conversations offline if needed

## Legal Considerations

### What You Can and Can't Say

**Be careful with:**
- Financial projections
- Guaranteed returns
- Investment advice
- Unverified claims
- Confidential information

**Always:**
- Be truthful
- Provide disclaimers
- Respect NDAs
- Follow securities laws
- Consult legal counsel when unsure

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How often should I update supporters?**
A: During funding: 2-3x per week. After graduation: 2-4x per month minimum.

**Q: What if I have nothing new to share?**
A: Share behind-the-scenes content, team spotlights, or industry news. If truly nothing, it's okay to skip.

**Q: Should I share bad news?**
A: Yes, transparency builds trust. Share problems along with solutions.

**Q: How long should updates be?**
A: Long enough to be informative, short enough to be engaging. Usually 200-500 words.

**Q: Can I delete old updates?**
A: Not recommended. They provide historical context and transparency.

**Q: What if supporters criticize my updates?**
A: Listen to feedback, respond professionally, and improve based on valid concerns.
