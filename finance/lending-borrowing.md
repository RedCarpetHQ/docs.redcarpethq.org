## Quick Links

**Lenders:**
- Deposit USDC to earn interest
- Auto-allocation across lending, stability pool, and insurance
- Risk-tiered interest rates
- Withdraw anytime (subject to utilization)

**Borrowers:**
- Use campaign tokens as collateral
- Borrow USDC at competitive rates
- Risk tier determines max LTV
- Monitor liquidation risk

For complete details, visit the [Finance Overview](finance/overview.md) page.
