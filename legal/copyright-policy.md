# Copyright and DMCA Policy

**Effective Date:** February 11, 2025

## What is RedCarpetHQ's Copyright Policy?

RedCarpetHQ has adopted a general policy regarding copyright in accordance with the Digital Millennium Copyright Act of 1998 (DMCA) and applicable international copyright laws. RedCarpetHQ removes material that is the subject of a compliant DMCA takedown notice. Section 512 of the DMCA provides the rules for reporting copyright infringement and for filing a counter-notification.

**If you believe your copyright is being infringed, you can report the alleged violation.**

**If your campaign was the subject of a takedown notice, you can submit a counter-notification.**

**If you've supported a campaign that was the subject of a takedown notice, you can learn what that means.**

In addition to complying with the DMCA safe harbor, RedCarpetHQ may in its sole discretion terminate user accounts of those who are repeat infringers of the intellectual property rights of others. RedCarpetHQ also reserves the right to terminate user accounts based on a single instance of infringement.

RedCarpetHQ does not control content hosted on third party websites or blockchain networks, and cannot remove content from sites or networks it does not own or control. If you are the copyright owner of content hosted on a third party site, and you have not authorized the use of your content, please contact the administrator of that website directly to have the content removed.

**Important Note on Blockchain:** Content and metadata stored on the blockchain (such as campaign token names, symbols, and certain campaign information) are immutable and cannot be removed once recorded. However, RedCarpetHQ can remove or disable access to campaign pages and associated content on our platform.

## How Do I Report a Copyright Violation?

If you're not sure whether material on RedCarpetHQ infringes on your copyright, please consult with an attorney before filing a DMCA notification. Copyright laws of the United States and other jurisdictions require you to consider copyright defenses, limitations, or exceptions, such as the fair use doctrine, before sending a notice. If you misrepresent that material infringes your copyright, you may be subject to liability for damages, including costs and attorneys' fees incurred by producers or other parties.

If you have a good faith belief that any materials accessible on or from https://www.redcarpethq.com/ (the "RedCarpetHQ Platform") infringe your copyright, you may request removal of those materials (or access to them) from the RedCarpetHQ Platform by filing a DMCA notification ("Take Down Notice") by contacting us at copyright@redcarpethq.com.

In accordance with Section 512 of the DMCA, your written notice must include substantially the following information (please note that all information must be submitted in English):

1. An electronic or physical signature of the person authorized to act on behalf of the owner of the copyright interest

2. A description of the copyrighted work that you claim has been infringed

3. A description of where the material that you claim is infringing is located on the RedCarpetHQ website, in a sufficiently precise manner to allow RedCarpetHQ to locate the material - please be as specific as possible, including:
   - The title of the work
   - The campaign URL
   - The type of media
   - Any other identifying information

4. Adequate information by which we can contact you (including your name, your postal address, your telephone number, and, if available and preferably, your email address)

5. The following statements:
   - "I hereby understand that under 17 U.S.C § 512(f) I may be liable for any damages, including costs and attorneys' fees, if I knowingly and materially misrepresent that reported material or activity is infringing"
   - "I have a good faith belief that the disputed use is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law (e.g., fair use)"
   - "I hereby state that the information in this notice is accurate and, under penalty of perjury, I am the copyright owner or authorized to act on behalf of the owner of an exclusive right under the copyright law that is allegedly infringed"
   - "I declare under penalty of perjury that I have considered whether the potential infringement is authorized as an exception or limitation under applicable law, such as fair use under U.S. Copyright Law"

## What Happens If I Receive a DMCA Notification About My Campaign?

If RedCarpetHQ receives a DMCA notice about your campaign, our policy is to remove the infringing material or disable access to your campaign until the dispute is resolved or the DMCA process is complete. RedCarpetHQ reserves full discretion to determine whether the campaign is hidden in full or in part.

You will receive an email from RedCarpetHQ attaching the claimant's Take Down Notice and providing instructions for filing a counter-notification if you wish to dispute it. RedCarpetHQ processes Take Down Notices and counter-notifications that it receives for adherence to legal formalities only; we cannot and will not adjudicate the validity of any claim of copyright infringement or if a defense like fair use may apply.

**Blockchain Considerations:** While we can hide or disable your campaign page on our platform, please note that certain information already recorded on the blockchain (such as token names, symbols, and transaction history) cannot be removed as blockchain records are immutable.

## How Do I File a Counter-Notification to Dispute the Claims?

If your campaign is the subject of a Take Down Notice and has been hidden or disabled, and you believe that your campaign or the material it includes is not infringing or that you have authorization or a right to post and use that material from the copyright owner or pursuant to law, you can submit a counter-notification to RedCarpetHQ's copyright agent by emailing us at copyright@redcarpethq.com.

Your counter-notification must contain the following information (please confirm these requirements with your legal counsel or see the U.S. Copyright Act, 17 U.S.C. §512(g)(3) for more information). Please also note that all information must be submitted in English:

1. Your physical or electronic signature

2. Identification of the material that has been removed or to which access has been disabled, and the location at which the material appeared before it was removed or access to it was disabled

3. "Under penalty of perjury, I have a good faith belief that the material was removed or disabled as a result of mistake or misidentification of the material"

4. Your name, address, telephone number, and a statement that you consent to the jurisdiction of the federal district court for the judicial district in which your address is located, or if your address is outside of the United States, for any judicial district in which the service provider may be found, and that you will accept service of process from the person who provided the Take Down Notice, or an agent of such person

## What Happens After I Submit a Counter-Notification?

After RedCarpetHQ receives your counter notification, we'll forward it to the person that submitted the Take Down Notice. If we do not receive a notice within 10 business days after forwarding the counter notification that the owner has filed an action seeking a court order to prevent alleged infringement, RedCarpetHQ may reinstate the campaign at issue in its sole discretion.

Access to your campaign or to the infringing material will remain disabled after we receive your counter-notification until the dispute is resolved or until 10 business days have passed without notification that the owner has filed an action seeking a court order. During this time, you will still have the option to message your supporters, edit your campaign (where permitted), or cancel your campaign.

## Will Anyone See My DMCA Notification or Counter-Notification?

Yes. RedCarpetHQ is committed to transparency when it comes to requests that disable or hide access to content on our platform. We use reasonable efforts to publish a copy of each Take Down Notice and counter notification on our site and on Lumen, a platform dedicated to ensuring transparency and accountability with respect to use (and abuse) of DMCA processes.

Personally-identifying information such as the claimant's name, email and telephone number will be removed from Take Down Notices and counter notifications before they are published. However, a claimant's or counter-claimant's address and company name (if provided) will be posted publicly. You consent to such posting when you submit your Take Down Notice or counter notification.

RedCarpetHQ also reserves the right to forward all materials received (including full contact details) to the opposing party in the DMCA dispute. This is essential for RedCarpetHQ's compliance with the DMCA.

## What Happens to My Contribution If the Campaign I Supported Is Subject to a Copyright Dispute?

If a campaign you've supported becomes the subject of a copyright dispute and RedCarpetHQ removes part or all of the campaign from public view, you'll get an email that explains the status and next steps. Any material that becomes subject to a copyright dispute will be removed or access disabled until the dispute is resolved or the DMCA process is complete.

Sometimes this is a very short period of time - please be patient and reach out directly to the producer if you are concerned. If a campaign or any of its contents are removed or hidden from view because of a dispute, you'll still be able to manage your contribution by going to the campaign page.

Please note that because RedCarpetHQ is only following legal processes and not making an assessment on the underlying claim of infringement, you shouldn't assume that the producer has engaged in an act of infringement, or that no infringement exists if a campaign is restored.

**For Active Campaigns:**
If we aren't able to re-post the campaign within 30 days, we will cancel it, and supporters will be able to claim refunds by burning their tokens. If the dispute is resolved or the DMCA process resolves within 30 days, the campaign will be restored to public view. We'll update you by email if any of these steps are taken.

**For Graduated Campaigns:**
If a graduated campaign (one that has successfully completed and tokens are tradable) is subject to a copyright dispute, the campaign page may be hidden, but tokens will remain in holders' wallets and may still be tradable on the marketplace. Blockchain records cannot be altered or removed.

## How Do I Report a Campaign for Patent Infringement?

RedCarpetHQ evaluates and takes action on claims of patent infringement in very limited circumstances. One of the reasons for this is that most of the time the reward that is the subject of the patent has not been manufactured when the campaign first launches.

Notwithstanding, if you believe that a campaign is offering an item or service that infringes a valid and enforceable patent, please submit the valid patent registration number and a court order finding infringement of the patent by the campaign to copyright@redcarpethq.com. Given the complexity of patents, we require a court order in order to process these claims.

Additionally, please be sure to include the following information in your message:

- URL of infringing campaign
- Your full legal name
- Your email address (please use company email address)
- Company name (optional)
- Company street address (optional)
- The following statements:
  - "I understand that RedCarpetHQ may provide third parties, including the affected user, with a copy of this complaint"
  - "The information in this complaint is true and correct and I am the patent holder or am authorized to act on the patent holder's behalf"

RedCarpetHQ will review claims of patent infringement and will remove from public view any campaign that is the subject of a court order finding infringement of a valid and enforceable patent.
