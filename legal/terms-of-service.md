RedCarpetHQ is not a part of this contract—the contract is a direct agreement between producers and their supporters, executed through smart contracts on the Ethereum blockchain. 1. **Producer Obligations.** During a campaign, and especially when a campaign is successfully funded, producers owe their supporters a high standard of effort, honest communication, and a dedication to bringing the project to life. They are also not purchasing securities or investment contracts. Every project is different. There may be changes or delays, and there's a chance something could happen that prevents the producer from being able to finish the project.

2. **Token-Based Support.** When you support a campaign, you receive ERC20 tokens representing your contribution. These tokens are:
   - Not securities or investment contracts
   - Not guarantees of financial return
   - Tradable on the RedCarpetHQ marketplace after campaign graduation
   - Potentially eligible for dividend distributions if the producer chooses to share revenue
   - Subject to smart contract execution and blockchain confirmation
   - Your sole responsibility to custody and secure

3. **Campaign Success and Graduation.** A campaign is considered successful if it reaches its floor funding goal by the deadline. Upon success:
   - The producer receives the raised funds (in USDC) to their designated wallet
   - Campaign tokens become tradable on the marketplace
   - The UnifiedVaultV3 is deployed for DeFi functionality
   - Supporters can trade, lend, or hold their tokens
   - The producer may distribute dividends to token holders

4. **If a Producer Cannot Complete Their Project.** If a producer is unable to complete their project, they must make a reasonable effort to find another way of bringing the project to the best possible conclusion for supporters. A producer in this position has remedied the situation and met their obligations to supporters if:

   - They work diligently and in good faith to bring the project to the best possible conclusion under the circumstances, in a timeframe that's communicated to supporters
   - They're able to demonstrate that they've used funds appropriately and made every reasonable effort to complete the project as promised
   - They've been honest, and have made no material misrepresentations in their communication to supporters
   - They offer to return any remaining funds to supporters (in proportion to the amounts contributed), or else explain how those funds will be used to complete the project in some alternate form

## Section 5. How Funding Works

This section goes over the details of supporting and creating campaigns—things like how money gets collected, whether contributions can be changed or canceled, and how tokens are distributed.

**These are the terms that apply when you support a campaign during a live campaign before the funding deadline:**

**You're only charged if the campaign reaches its fundraising goal.** You'll provide your payment information and approve the transaction when you contribute, and your USDC will be transferred to the campaign smart contract. Your funds will only be released to the producer if, at the time of the campaign's funding deadline, the campaign has reached its floor funding goal. The amount you contributed is the amount that will be collected. If the campaign doesn't reach its floor goal, you can claim a full refund from the campaign contract, no funds will be released to the producer, and the campaign will be marked as FAILED.

**You receive tokens immediately upon contribution.** When you contribute USDC to a campaign, you immediately receive campaign tokens to your wallet. The number of tokens you receive is determined by the token price set by the producer. These tokens are locked and non-transferable until the campaign graduates (successfully completes).

**Subject to certain exceptions, you can claim a refund before the campaign deadline.** After you contribute to a campaign, you can claim a refund while the campaign is live by burning your tokens and receiving your USDC back from the campaign contract, except in the following scenarios:
   - After the campaign has reached its ceiling (maximum funding goal)
   - After the campaign deadline has passed
   - If the campaign has been cancelled by the producer or screener

**Grace Period.** If a campaign reaches between its floor and ceiling goals, it enters a grace period of up to 14 days. During this time:
   - The producer can extend the deadline once
   - Supporters can continue to contribute
   - The campaign can still reach its ceiling
   - If the grace period expires without reaching the ceiling, the campaign graduates with the amount raised

**These are the terms that apply when you're creating and running a campaign:**

**You can refund individual contributions if you want.** After your campaign has been funded, you can manually refund a supporter's contribution by sending USDC directly to their wallet. If you fully refund a supporter's contribution, you should communicate with them about burning their tokens, though you have no technical ability to force this.

**Campaign funds are transferred directly to your wallet.** RedCarpetHQ does not charge platform fees on campaign funding. You will receive 100% of the funds raised (minus blockchain gas fees paid to network validators).

**Some contributions may fail to process.** Because some blockchain transactions can fail—for instance, due to insufficient gas, network congestion, or wallet issues—the amount of funding you receive may not be equal to the full amount attempted by supporters (less fees).

**Prepare for potential delays.** Don't assume you'll be able to launch your campaign when you want; there could be a reason we're not able to approve it immediately upon submission, or a problem may arise that takes time to resolve. Don't assume you'll be able to immediately access your funding; there may be a delay between the end of a successful campaign and the finalization transaction that releases funds. Don't take any actions in reliance on collecting any of the money contributed by a certain time.

**Blockchain Transactions are Final.** Once funds are released to your wallet through the smart contract, the transaction is final and cannot be reversed. Ensure your wallet address is correct before launching your campaign.
