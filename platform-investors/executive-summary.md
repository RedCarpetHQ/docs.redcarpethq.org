# Executive Summary

## RedCarpetHQ: The AI-Powered Exchange for Tokenized Entertainment Profits

---

### The Opportunity

Entertainment is a $2.5 trillion global industry, yet its financing remains archaic: opaque waterfalls, gatekept by studios, and entirely illiquid for private investors. RedCarpetHQ is building the first decentralized exchange and DeFi protocol purpose-built for entertainment intellectual property (IP).

We do not run a crowdfunding platform. We operate a financial infrastructure layer that transforms film, TV, and entertainment projects into **tokenized, tradeable, yield-bearing assets** — complete with native secondary markets, automated lending, algorithmic risk pricing, and direct profit distribution.

### What We Built

| Component | Description |
|---|---|
| **Campaign Engine** | All-or-nothing fundraising with Floor/Ceiling targets. Zero platform fees on raises. |
| **Tokenization Layer** | Every successful project mints a unique ERC-20 token representing economic participation. |
| **Embedded DeFi** | Each token carries a UnifiedVault (ERC-4626) enabling USDC lending/borrowing against the token. |
| **AI Risk Oracle** | RiskOracleV3 continuously scores tokens into GREEN/YELLOW/RED tiers based on price integrity, wash trading, and vault health. |
| **VWAP Marketplace** | Escrow-based peer-to-peer trading with volume-weighted average pricing — resistant to manipulation. |
| **Automated Profit Distribution** | Revenue from streaming, box office, and licensing flows pro-rata to token holders via on-chain snapshots. |

### Revenue Model

- **2.5% Trading Fee** on every marketplace transaction
- **Protocol Interest Share** from vault lending activity
- **Fee Distribution**: 40% Treasury, 40% Trading Contests, 10% Vault, 10% Producer

The model aligns all stakeholders: traders earn rewards, lenders earn APY, producers earn passive fee income, and the protocol earns sustainably without taxing fundraising.
