## Key Dependencies & Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Smart contract vulnerability | Multiple audits, bug bounties, circuit breakers, insurance fund |
| Low initial trading volume | Trading contests recycle 40% of fees; producer incentives; zero campaign fees attract creators |
| Regulatory uncertainty | Tokens represent project participation, not securities; decentralized screener model; legal counsel engaged |
| Producer onboarding friction | Screening process streamlined; dedicated producer success team; templates and guides |
| Base network growth | Aligned with Coinbase ecosystem; low fees attract retail; easily portable to other L2s |
